The interior of a warm, lived-in home KITCHEN on a Mediterranean evening.

GEOMETRY: a modest kitchen along one wall — a worn butcher-block wood counter running the length of it, a deep white ceramic sink with a simple brass tap, a small gas hob, pale hand-glazed square tiles on the splashback with slightly uneven grout. Above the counter, two OPEN wooden shelves holding mismatched everyday crockery, glass jars of grains and spices, a stack of small ceramic bowls, a chipped mug or two. Below the counter, plain wooden cupboard doors, one slightly ajar. To one side a tall old fridge with a few things stuck to it. In the middle of the room a small wooden table with two mismatched chairs, one pulled out at an angle. A large window above the sink.

THE LIVED-IN DETAIL: a wooden board with a knife left on it, a half-full bottle of olive oil, a bowl of lemons, a tea towel thrown over the oven rail, a small potted herb on the windowsill, a pair of reading glasses left on the table, crumbs not yet wiped. Nothing is styled and nothing is put away perfectly. This is a real kitchen at the end of a day, not a showroom.

LIGHT: low golden late-evening sun coming in almost horizontally through the window above the sink, throwing long warm light across the counter and one wall and leaving the far corners in soft warm shadow. One small warm practical bulb over the counter, just switched on and barely competing with the daylight. Soft honest bounce off the pale tiles. Warm, quiet, low-contrast, nothing crushed.

MATERIALS: knife-scarred wood, worn brass, glazed ceramic with real glaze variation, glass, old paint, everything with honest wear.

OPTICS: Arri Alexa Mini LF with anamorphic primes, 2.39:1; camera at standing chest height, about 1.4 m, a few steps back from the counter looking along it toward the window; centre sharp, edges gently soft; fine organic 35mm grain.

STYLE: photorealistic contemporary domestic cinematography, warm, quiet and understated. No text, no brand names, no labels on any jar or bottle, no signage, no artwork with writing.
