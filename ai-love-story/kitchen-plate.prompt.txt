Master location plate, completely empty of people, 2.39:1.

The hot line of a working PROFESSIONAL RESTAURANT KITCHEN, seen from inside, looking along the line toward the pass.

GEOMETRY: a narrow stainless-steel corridor. Down one side runs the cooking line — a six-burner gas range with heavy iron grates, a flat-top griddle, a chargrill, a salamander above them, all scorched and scrubbed. Facing it across the walkway, a stainless prep counter with under-counter fridge drawers and a row of small steel gastronorm inserts set into the top holding prepped ingredients. At the far end of the line, THE PASS: a stainless shelf under a row of amber HEAT LAMPS, with a metal ticket rail running above it and a printer at one end. Overhead, a low stainless extraction hood the length of the range, and a rail of hanging pans and ladles. Quarry-tiled floor with a drain and a rubber safety mat. A hand sink, a knife rack, a bain-marie, sheet trays stacked on a speed rack.

THE WORKING DETAIL: pans left on the flat top, a pot at a rolling boil throwing steam up into the hood, a squeeze bottle and a stack of small plates near the pass, a wiped-down but stained surface everywhere, blue tape on lids, a side towel over a rail, tongs resting across a pan. Real wear: dented steel, scorched grates, scratched surfaces, honest grease at the seams. This is a kitchen in the middle of service, not a showroom.

LIGHT: cool white overhead strip lighting the length of the line, hard and functional, throwing short shadows; and against it the warm amber pool of the HEAT LAMPS over the pass at the far end, plus live orange flame under the pans and a red glow from the salamander. Cool overhead, warm at the pass — the two are always in tension in this room. Steam catches the light and drifts through it.

MATERIALS: brushed and scratched stainless everywhere, cast iron, scorched enamel, quarry tile, rubber matting.

OPTICS: Arri Alexa Mini LF with anamorphic primes, 2.39:1; camera at standing chest height, about 1.4 m, in the middle of the walkway looking down the length of the line toward the pass; centre sharp, edges gently soft; fine organic 35mm grain.

STYLE: photorealistic contemporary kitchen cinematography, hot, hard-working and unglamorous. No people. No text, no brand names, no writing on any ticket, label or tape, no signage, no menus.
