6 shots, 15 seconds total, 2.39:1, anamorphic. Hard cuts. Slow motion ONLY inside SHOT 1. One continuous story: two strangers in a lift, a melody neither of them can place, and the moment they finally look at each other.

SCENE ENGINE (production spine)
GOAL: he wants nothing and is asking for nothing — he is simply riding a lift at the end of an ordinary day. She wants to get home. Neither is looking for anyone.
OBSTACLE: they are strangers with no reason to speak, in a box that will open in fifteen seconds; she is inside her own music and cannot hear the room.
TACTIC: he does not speak to her, does not look at her, does not try anything. He hums, to himself, without meaning to.
REVERSAL: the thing that finally reaches her is the one thing he was not doing on purpose.
VALUE SHIFT: two strangers sharing a lift → why do I know that tune → I have known this person before → and neither of them has the words for it. This is the last scene of a film in which they have found each other across six lifetimes and never once needed to speak.
