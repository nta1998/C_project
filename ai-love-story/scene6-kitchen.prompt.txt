OBSTACLE: it is the middle of service, nobody has a spare hand, and she does not know him.
TACTIC: she stops trying to do it alone, picks the bowl up, walks the length of the line, and asks.
REVERSAL: this is the film's reversal, and its beginning. Five lifetimes of grand impossible reaching — a leap at a departing liner, a crowd of a thousand, a viaduct over the sea — and what actually starts it is a woman with a bowl of olives asking a stranger for a hand on a Tuesday night.
