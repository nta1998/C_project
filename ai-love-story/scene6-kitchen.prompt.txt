7 shots, 18 seconds total, 2.39:1, anamorphic. Hard cuts. NO slow motion anywhere in this film. THREE spoken lines, all of them hers. One continuous story: a bartender walks into a hot kitchen with a sealed bucket of olives and a reason to talk to the cook — and the punchline is not the one anybody expects.

SCENE ENGINE (production spine)
GOAL: she wants the bucket opened, and she wants a minute of his time; he wants to be useful to her the second she asks.
OBSTACLE: none worth the name. This is the easiest thing that happens to either of them all night.
TACTIC: she brings him a job only he can do and stays to watch him do it. He says yes before she has finished asking.
REVERSAL: the joke, and the whole point. Everything about how she arrives reads as a woman who came in for HIM — and then she opens her mouth and offers him her sister. He does not get a single word out.
VALUE SHIFT: two strangers → she is charmed and she is not hiding it → he is delighted to be asked → she is sizing him up → and she was scouting for somebody else the entire time. This is the last scene of the film and the first minute of the story: it did not go the way she planned.
