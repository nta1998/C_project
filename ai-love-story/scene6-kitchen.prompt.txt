6 shots, 15 seconds total, 2.39:1, anamorphic. Hard cuts. NO slow motion anywhere in this film. One continuous story: an ordinary evening in a kitchen, and a man hands a woman a bowl of olives.

SCENE ENGINE (production spine)
GOAL: nobody in this scene wants anything. She is finishing something at the counter; he is putting olives in a bowl. That is all that is happening, and it is the point.
OBSTACLE: none. There is nothing between them and there has not been for a long time.
TACTIC: none. Neither of them is trying.
REVERSAL: this is the film's reversal. Across every era before this one they have been separated — by a liner, by a crowd, by a viaduct, by an arena — and every reunion has been one held breath and one impossible reach. Here they simply live in the same kitchen, and the reach is a bowl passed across a counter without either of them looking up.
VALUE SHIFT: the audience arrives braced for another near-miss → nothing happens → and that is the happy ending. This is the last scene of a film in which two people found each other across six lifetimes and never once got to keep each other. They kept each other.
