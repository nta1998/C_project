9 shots, 18 seconds total, 2.39:1, anamorphic. Hard cuts — fast-cut rhythm, every cut lands on an action. Slow motion ONLY inside SHOT 9. One continuous story: a young fighter takes down a giant three times his size, and the woman who must not show she cares gives him one look.

SCENE ENGINE (production spine)
GOAL: he must put the giant on the ground; she must get through this without anyone seeing what she feels.
OBSTACLE: the giant outweighs him many times over and cannot be fought head-on; her every reaction is watched by a full amphitheatre.
TACTIC: he does not attack the giant's front — he goes UP the crossed chains on its back and uses the giant's own mass against it. She holds her face still and lets only the smallest things through: a caught breath, a lean of a few centimeters.
REVERSAL: the size that made the giant unbeatable is the same mass that puts him down when his own momentum is turned.
VALUE SHIFT: a boy sent out to die → a boy who is not afraid → he is going to win → he wins, and does not kill. Her verdict: a stranger on the sand → I cannot watch this → I am not going to look away.

REFERENCE DEFINITIONS
@arena: a vast sun-bleached stone amphitheatre at high midday — an open oval floor of pale dusty sand marked with old craters and drag scars; a tall perimeter wall of plastered stone in faded red-and-white bands; packed tiers of roaring crowd above it and an arched upper gallery with a pale colonnade against a bleached white sky; set into one side and raised above the wall, a marble ROYAL LOGGIA with slender fluted columns, a carved balustrade, a stretched awning and a gilded high-backed throne with a scrolled gilded armrest. Geometry, materials, crowd and light only. Reference.
@He_arena: young fighter — face, hair, build and wardrobe match the reference exactly in every frame: fitted worn muscled bronze cuirass over a short pleated leather war-skirt with a frayed hem, leather baldric across the chest, bronze bracers on both forearms, sandals laced high up the shins; lean athletic build, sun-darkened arms and legs, short dark hair, short dark beard, no helmet. Reference.
@She_arena: young woman — face, hair and wardrobe match the reference exactly in every frame: floor-length ivory-white draped linen gown gathered under the bust, narrow gold cord belt, one bare shoulder with a gold brooch on the other, a wide engraved GOLD CUFF BRACELET on her right wrist, small gold drop earrings; long dark-brown hair dressed high in a braided coil with fine gold pins and a slim gold circlet, soft tendrils at the temples. Reference.
@giant: the arena executioner — a colossus, matching the reference exactly in every frame: enormous soft-heavy build with a huge round belly and no muscle definition, thick heavy arms and column legs; his head FULLY covered by a peaked leather executioner's hood with two round stitched eye holes and a flaring skirt over the shoulders, the crown and nape blank stitched leather, NO face ever visible; TWO massive chains of fist-sized links crossing his torso and his bare back in a big X, lying flat and tight with no loose ends; a ragged jagged-hemmed hide wrap at the hips; cloth-wrapped sandals; a plain single-bladed executioner's axe with a broad crescent head in his RIGHT hand. Reference.
@sketch: the height comparison drawing. It governs ONE thing and nothing else — the size ratio between @giant and @He_arena. Its line-art style, its white background and its poses never appear in the film. Reference.

TECHNICAL BLOCK
Cinematic photoreal, ultra filmic antique arena at midday. 21:9 — 35mm anamorphic film character: horizontal squeeze and compression, oval elliptical bokeh with stretched highlights, curved barrel edge distortion, subtle chromatic aberration toward the edges — all subtle and moderate; NO lens flares, NO streaks, NO floating bokeh circles. SOFT COLOR GRADE: sun-bleached warm sand and cream, lifted soft blacks, low contrast — the same grade as the other eras of this film carried into hard daylight. Shot on 35mm, fine period grain. Natural 180-degree shutter cadence — directional motion blur on fast moves, readable impact poses at the holds; no ghosting, no duplicated limbs, no strobing. ALL HANDHELD, organic, never chaotic. SFX only.

PACE: every shot lands its beat and cuts. Each cut arrives ON an action, never after it — no dead frames, no slowing down to fill time. The piece runs 18 seconds exactly and ends on the frame described in SHOT 9.

LAWS:
- THE SCALE, the single most important law: @giant stands THREE TIMES the height of @He_arena, exactly as locked in @sketch. In every frame that holds both of them, the top of @He_arena's head reaches only @giant's MID-THIGH — not his waist, not his chest. @giant's hand is as wide as @He_arena's torso; his forearm is thicker than @He_arena's body. This extreme ratio is intentional and is never softened toward realistic proportions in any shot, at any distance, in any framing. If @He_arena's head reaches @giant's waist or higher, the shot is wrong.
- CAST LOCK: exactly THREE named figures exist in this film — @He_arena, @She_arena and @giant — and each appears exactly ONCE in any frame, never doubled. SHOT 1 holds @giant and @He_arena. SHOTS 2, 5 and 9 hold @She_arena alone in the loggia. SHOTS 3, 4, 6, 7 hold @He_arena and @giant. SHOT 8 holds @He_arena alone. Everyone else anywhere in frame is an anonymous crowd member in the far tiers.
- LOCATION LOCK: all nine shots take place in the SAME @arena: the same red-and-white banded wall, the same packed tiers and colonnade above, the same marble loggia with its awning and gilded throne, the same cratered sand underfoot.
- LIGHT CONTINUITY: ONE sun, high and almost vertical overhead, hard and unfiltered, identical in every shot and across every hard cut — short hard-edged shadows straight down, blazing sand, a bleached near-white sky, fine dust hanging and catching the light. The loggia sits under its awning in open shade with the same sun beyond it. No cloud, no time passing, no change of key anywhere in the film.
- THE HOOD: @giant's face is NEVER visible in any frame. The hood stays on through the run, the climb, the throw and the fall — only the two round eye holes and blank stitched leather. Front markers (eye holes, mouth seam, the face of the hood) appear ONLY when he faces camera; from behind, only the blank stitched crown and nape.
- IDENTITY UNDER MOTION: both leads' faces stay photoreal, sharp and identity-locked in EVERY frame, and the same bone structure, proportions and features carry through fast motion, directional motion blur and slow motion alike. In the slow-motion passage — SHOT 9 — slow motion changes ONLY the speed of time: @She_arena's face holds the exact geometry of her reference entering the ramp, through it and leaving it. Linen, tendrils and dust are the things that stretch; the face is not.
- SHE STAYS IN THE BOX: @She_arena never leaves the royal loggia and never stands on the sand. She is seen only from inside or just outside the box, the balustrade and the gilded armrest in frame with her, the arena always below her frame line.
- HE DOES NOT KILL: the fight ends with @giant flat on the sand and @He_arena standing over him holding the sword low. No blood anywhere in the film, no killing blow, no wound.
- FACE RULE: a lead's visible face fills at least a THIRD of frame height, chest-up or closer, fully inside the frame; at distance a lead reads from behind or in profile; eyes never look into the lens.
- LIVING FACE: the eyes always WORK at a task, never pose; a blink lands ONLY on a change of thought, a few per shot; never a frozen glassy stare.
- LEVEL CAMERA: horizon level, zero roll, verticals upright.
- LIVING CROWD: the tiers are packed edge to edge in every frame that shows them, at the SAME density from the first frame to the last — standing, shouting, arms up, leaning over the rail, every figure different, all of them small in frame and never individually featured. They react to the fight, never to the lens.

SHOT 1 — 0.0–2.0s — THE SAND — geography and scale
84° diagonal field of view, low on the sand. First frame: @giant is ALREADY standing mid-arena, filling the frame from the sand to well above the perimeter wall, axe low at his shin, hood turned away, the packed tiers and the bleached sky behind him; @He_arena stands small at the far edge of the sand, a third of the giant's height, sword low. The camera pushes slowly in along the ground. Dust drifts across the gap between them. This shot establishes the ratio: read the giant's mid-thigh against the top of the young man's head.

SHOT 2 — 2.0–3.5s — HER IN THE BOX
Hard cut. 47° diagonal field of view (a 50mm-equivalent look) from across the arena: @She_arena alone in the marble loggia, seated on the gilded throne under the awning, one hand resting easy on the scrolled armrest, the gold cuff catching the shade. Composed and still, eye-line forward and slightly DOWN toward the sand below the frame line. ACTING TASK — HER: task — watch a public event as a public person; give the crowd nothing. Calm, quietly attentive, warmth held in check. One unhurried blink.

SHOT 3 — 3.5–5.0s — THE RUN
Hard cut. 84° diagonal field of view, low from the sand directly BEHIND @He_arena, handheld pursuit energy: he runs away from camera across the open sand toward @giant, who stands mid-arena against the backlit colonnade, dust rolling around his sandals, the axe in his fist. Sand kicks off @He_arena's heels. His eyes are locked on the chain X crossing the giant's back from the first stride. He looks tiny against the mass ahead of him.

SHOT 4 — 5.0–7.0s — THE CLIMB
Hard cut. 75° diagonal field of view, camera jolting with each stride: two, three driving strides, @He_arena plants a sandal on the raised rim of an old axe crater and LEAVES THE GROUND — up onto the giant's back, both hands catching the taut crossed chain runs, boots finding the links, climbing hand over hand up the bare back toward the blank stitched nape. @giant's hood is turned away, scanning the far side of the arena; he feels the weight late and starts to turn. From behind, only the blank crown and nape and the chain X are visible.

SHOT 5 — 7.0–8.5s — SHE ALMOST GIVES HERSELF AWAY
Hard cut. 29° diagonal field of view (an 85mm-equivalent look) from well back, a tight CLOSE-UP on @She_arena in three-quarter, the crowd a far wall of bokeh beyond the balustrade. The roar of the amphitheatre SNAPS into a single shocked "OOOH". ACTING TASK — HER: task — do not be seen caring; her breath catches and holds, her body eases a few centimeters FORWARD to see, a light quiet lean toward the arena, nothing in the face but a fraction more focus in the eyes. Her hand on the gilded armrest does NOT move and does NOT close. She is afraid for the one down there and will not let it show.

SHOT 6 — 8.5–11.0s — THE THROW
Hard cut. 84° diagonal field of view, low and wide on the sand. @giant reaches back over his own shoulder with one enormous hand and closes it around @He_arena — and @He_arena does not resist it: he rides the pull, plants both sandals against the giant's shoulder and drives with his legs, turning the giant's own reaching momentum over the top. The colossus goes over sideways and SLAMS flat onto the sand. The ground jumps, a wall of dust bursts outward and rolls past the lens, the axe bounces free of his hand. @He_arena is thrown clear.

SHOT 7 — 11.0–13.0s — THE SWORD
Hard cut. 75° diagonal field of view, close and low, dust still hanging: @He_arena hits the sand, rolls once and comes up onto one knee with his hand already closing on the fallen xiphos. He stands. He walks two steps and stops over @giant, who lies flat and unmoving, chest heaving, the hood face-up to the vertical sun. @He_arena holds the sword LOW at his side and does not raise it. Chest heaving, dust on his shoulders. He has won and he is not going to finish it.

SHOT 8 — 13.0–15.0s — HE LOOKS UP
Hard cut. 47° diagonal field of view from ahead-left, low: @He_arena's face LARGE in three-quarter against the blazing sky, sweat and dust on him, breath still tearing. He lifts his eyes off the sand and raises them UP and past the camera, to the loggia. ACTING TASK — HIM: task — find one person in a full amphitheatre and check whether she is still watching; the eyes climb, settle, and stop. The roar around him goes on and he does not hear it.

SHOT 9 — 15.0–18.0s — HER ONE LOOK — slow motion
Hard cut, whole shot in smooth slow motion. 29° diagonal field of view (an 85mm-equivalent look) from well back, a CLOSE-UP on @She_arena in three-quarter, hard sun modelling her face, the crowd a soft wall behind her, the arena below her frame line. ACTING TASK — HER: task — give him one thing that only he will read, in front of ten thousand people; her eye-line comes forward and slightly DOWN toward the sand, never into the lens; the held breath finally goes out of her; the corner of her mouth lifts into a warm private half-smile, and one slow blink lands on it. Then she rises and turns away — and the smile is still on her face as she goes. Final frame HELD: her face in three-quarter mid-turn, the smile holding, a loose tendril crossing her cheek, dust suspended in the shaded air of the loggia. This exact frame is the last frame of the film, at 18.0s.

PHYSICS
Everything obeys the mass difference. @He_arena moves fast, light and precise — real acceleration in the sprint, a real planted takeoff off the crater rim, real grip and real strain climbing the chains. @giant moves slow and heavy: he turns late, his belly and arms carry visible inertia, and when he goes down it is dead weight — the sand craters under him, the impact jumps the ground, dust bursts outward in a low rolling wall and hangs. The throw is honest leverage, not strength: the giant's own reaching momentum carried over the top. The axe bounces once and lies still. The chains swing and settle with real iron weight. Linen, tendrils and the awning move with the hot air. Every figure solid, every contact shadow correct on the sand.

SFX only: the amphitheatre ROARS — a packed antique crowd, shouting, stamping on stone, a rolling wall of voices. Under it: @He_arena's sandals hammering the sand, his breath tearing, the iron rattle of the chains taking his weight, the giant's slow heavy shifting, the crowd's single shocked "OOOH" as he goes up. The THROW lands as one enormous body impact — a deep ground thud, a burst of dust, the axe clattering. Then the crowd EXPLODES. Through the slow-motion last shot everything dips muffled and far away, leaving one close breath and the hot wind moving the awning — and the piece ends inside it. No dialogue, no narration, no music of any kind. 35mm grain, sun-bleached historical-epic realism.
