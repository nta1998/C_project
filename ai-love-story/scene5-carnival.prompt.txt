9 shots, 18 seconds total, 2.39:1, anamorphic. Hard cuts — fast-cut rhythm, every cut lands on an action. Slow motion ONLY inside SHOT 7 and inside SHOT 9. One continuous story: a masked carnival, a man who has seen her once and never met her, a crowd that keeps taking her away — and one fingertip.

SCENE ENGINE (production spine — the whole piece plays this chain)
GOAL (fixed, never changes): reach HER through the crowd before it closes over her again.
OBSTACLE (global): a thousand celebrating bodies between them, and a carnival that keeps catching him into its games; (local): the dancing ring, the wall of capes, the torch-bearers.
TACTIC: hunt the ONE colour that is hers. He never forces a path — he weaves, sidesteps, apologises with his hands, and keeps the burgundy in his eye as long as the crowd allows.
REVERSAL: the crowd that hid her all night is the same crowd that finally parts and leaves them a meter apart, with nothing left to hide behind.
VALUE SHIFT: a stranger in a mask → a man chasing a colour → a man who has stopped breathing → they are two people, and the whole square disappears. Her verdict: I am not looking for anyone → I know exactly who that is.

TECHNICAL BLOCK
Cinematic photoreal, ultra filmic old-Italian night carnival. 21:9 — 35mm anamorphic film character: horizontal squeeze and compression, oval elliptical bokeh with stretched highlights on the lantern strings and torch flames, curved barrel edge distortion, subtle chromatic aberration toward the edges — all subtle and moderate; NO lens flares, NO streaks, NO floating bokeh circles, no out-of-focus objects parked in front of the lens. SOFT COLOR GRADE: warm festival gold on stone and skin, cream highlights, lifted soft blacks, low contrast — soft and faded, the same grade as the daylight scenes of this film carried into night. Shot on 35mm, fine period grain, gentle halation on the practicals. Natural 180-degree shutter cadence — directional motion blur on fast moves, readable poses at holds; no ghosting, no duplicated limbs, no strobing. ALL HANDHELD, organic, never chaotic. SFX only.

PACE: every shot lands its beat and cuts. Each cut arrives ON an action, never after it — no dead frames, no held breath at the end of a shot, no slowing down to fill time. The piece runs 18 seconds exactly and ends on the frame described in SHOT 9.

LAWS:
- CAST LOCK: exactly TWO named people exist in this film — @He_carnival and @She_carnival — and each appears exactly ONCE in any frame, never doubled, never twinned in the crowd. SHOT 1, SHOT 3, SHOT 5 and SHOT 6 contain @He_carnival and no other named person. SHOT 4 and SHOT 7 contain @She_carnival alone. SHOT 2 holds @He_carnival in the near frame with @She_carnival glimpsed at distance. SHOTS 8 and 9 contain both. Everyone else in every shot is an anonymous masked reveller.
- LOCATION LOCK: all nine shots take place in the SAME @carnival piazza: the same palazzo facade and portal, the same arcades and columns, the same fountain, the same campanile, the same lantern strings overhead and the same paving underfoot, throughout.
- LIGHT CONTINUITY: every source is a practical that exists in @carnival — the criss-crossing lantern strings overhead, torches and iron wall-lanterns on the facades, the palazzo's glowing windows and portal, the ground spark fountains, the firework blooms. Warm festival gold washes the square from above and from the facades and stays the SAME warmth, the same level and the same direction across every hard cut. Every face and every mask stays readable — no crushed shadows anywhere, and no daylight ever leaks into this night. The firework blooms pulse the square a stop brighter for a beat and fall back; that pulse is the only light change in the film, and it never changes the colour of the key.
- THE ONLY BURGUNDY: in the entire packed piazza exactly TWO people wear red or burgundy, and they are @He_carnival and @She_carnival. Every other costume, gown, mask, cape, doublet and banner in every frame is emerald, sapphire, deep violet, teal, gold, silver, ochre or cream. Her red-burgundy gown is the one colour in the square that belongs to nobody else, and it is how he finds her.
- THE MASKS STAY ON: both masks are worn in every frame, narrow and high on the face — eyebrows fully visible above the mask edge, nose, cheeks, mouth and jaw completely uncovered, eyes fully open and readable through the openings. Neither mask is ever removed, lifted, pushed up onto the forehead or lowered onto the nose.
- IDENTITY UNDER MOTION: both faces stay photoreal, sharp and identity-locked in EVERY frame, and the same bone structure, proportions and features carry through fast motion, directional motion blur and slow motion alike. In the two slow-motion passages — SHOT 7 and SHOT 9 — slow motion changes ONLY the speed of time: entering the ramp, all the way through it, and leaving it, each face holds the exact geometry of its reference. Hair, silk, sleeves and sparks are the things that stretch; the faces are not.
- HE WALKS IN A HURRY: @He_carnival never runs anywhere in this scene and never breaks into a running gait. He moves on quick, light, eager strides — weaving through gaps, turning his shoulders sideways to slip past, lifting a hand in apology, giving way, never shoving anyone, never aggressive. Urgency lives in his pace and his eyes, never in force.
- SHE IS UNACCOMPANIED: @She_carnival walks alone through the throng at an unhurried pace, letting the crowd carry and conceal her. She is not dancing with anyone, not on anyone's arm, and no one is escorting her.
- FACE RULE: a lead's visible face fills at least a THIRD of frame height, chest-up or closer, fully inside the frame; at distance a lead reads from behind, in profile, or by the burgundy alone; eyes never look into the lens.
- LIVING FACE: the eyes always WORK at a task, never pose; a blink lands ONLY on a change of thought, slow and soft, a few per shot; never a frozen glassy stare.
- PORTRAIT DISTANCE: close framings come from a longer lens at distance — natural proportions, no wide-angle facial distortion.
- LEVEL CAMERA: horizon level, zero roll, verticals upright.
- LIVING CROWD: the piazza is PACKED in every frame and the density is the SAME at 0.0s as at 18.0s. At any moment dozens of anonymous masked revellers are visible and every one of them is MID-ACTION: dancing in rings on the open stone, strolling in masked pairs, clustering and laughing by the arcades and the fountain, raising glasses on the palazzo steps, applauding the fireworks, threading through with torches. Every body is solid and whole; every face, mask, build and costume is different from every other — bauta and colombina masks, gilded full-face masks, tricorn hats, hooded capes, jesters with bells, plague-doctor masks, powdered wigs, feathered headdresses. Nobody stands frozen, and nobody looks at the leads or at the lens.

Torch flames and lantern strings move with the night breeze; ground sparks arc and die on the paving; capes and gowns swing with their own mass; every figure is solid and every contact shadow is correct on the stone.
